Rules

  • Long trips go in base, lighter or recovery weeks. Never in a taper or a peak block.
  • Short trips start after the Friday long run. Saturday is rest anyway.
  • No trips in the 3 weeks before Plovdiv, in Bairi race week, or in the 2 weeks before Vitosha.
  • Marathon block (15 Feb – 28 Mar): weekends only.
  • Vitosha build (17 May – 13 Jun): only trail weekends that fit the back-to-back runs.
  • Public holidays are work days (double pay). Keep trips off them where you can.
  • Plan trip 5 before June.
  • Buffer: one Saturday a month stays open, with no plans (Sunday is family day). Spontaneous things go there. If something new comes up, move a plan, don't stack it.
  • No social night before a race. Late Thursdays are fine only when Friday is not a long run day.
