Counts in the totals only, with no breakdown.

How it measures: Overland sends your location in the background, and the back end finds every stop of 3 minutes or more. Name a stop once and every visit there, past and future, counts for it. Name your home first: leaving it and coming back is what makes a trip. Runs from your watch name their trip by themselves, even with the phone at home. The buttons at the top mark when you start getting ready, which no location can show. "Plan for" is the time you stay inside 4 times out of 5; with fewer than 3 trips it is the longest one so far.
